KOKORO WEAR (心・衣)

Brand Art Direction, Color Tokens, Dynamic Lighting Guardrails & AI Prompt Formula for Kokoro Cat Cafe NYC in partnership with Anjellicle Cats Rescue NYC.

1. Brand Essence & Visual Philosophy

Merges Japanese textile philosophy with the utility and pace of downtown NYC. Unposed moments of feline companionship paired with streetwear utility.

  • 間 (Ma): Deliberate negative space and breathing room in garment cuts and framing.
  • 侘寂 (Wabi-Sabi): Tactile slub yarn, raw unbleached fibers, washed denim, natural creasing.
  • 青海波 (Seigaiha): Concentric wave arches translated into feline ear and pocket geometry.
  • 襤褸 (Boro): Visible mending, contrast sashiko stitches, framed canvas photo-patches.

2. The 6 Core Color Tokens

#1A2B3C Indigo
#75848F Slate Grey
#9D8870 Natural Tan
#F4EFE6 Kinari Ecru
#C85A32 Tabby Ochre
#BA3B2E Hanko Red

3. Dynamic Lighting & Cinematography (Anti-Stiffness)

Eliminate flat catalog lighting. Use dynamic high-contrast chiaroscuro, Venetian blind zebra stripes, fire escape grid shadows, and low raking sunbeams that carve out textile texture.

4. Strict Guardrails: On-Brand vs Off-Brand

ON-BRAND (Do)
  • Unposed kinetic movement
  • Real rescue cats in natural poses
  • 35mm film grain & lens flare
  • Weathered Lower East Side cues
OFF-BRAND (Never)
  • Stiff mannequin stares
  • Seamless white studio cycs
  • Humans wearing fake cat ears
  • Cats wearing clothes or hats
