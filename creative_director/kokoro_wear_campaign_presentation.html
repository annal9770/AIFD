Brand System & Engine

KOKORO WEAR (心・衣)

Creative Director Brand Rules, Asset System, and AI Prompting Specifications for Kokoro Cat Cafe NYC Spring 2027 Fashion Launch.

01. Brand Essence & Contrast

Merges traditional Japanese textile heritage (Wabi-Sabi, Ma, Sashiko, Aizome) with downtown NYC streetwear utility. Clean architectural cuts paired with intimate, hand-stitched feline gestures. Proceeds directly fund shelter rescue.

02. The Combined Palette Tokens

#1A2B3C Dark Indigo
#75848F Slate Grey
#9D8870 Natural Tan
#F4EFE6 Kinari Ecru
#C85A32 Tabby Ochre
#BA3B2E Hanko Vermilion

03. Typography Rules

  • Michroma: Uppercase headlines, tracking +0.12em.
  • Shippori Mincho: Vertical Japanese script (心・衣, 猫青海波).
  • Space Grotesk: Garment specs, GSM weights, NYC addresses.
