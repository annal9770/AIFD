Atmospheric Quality Alert: New York Sector

Live atmospheric telemetry is continuously sampled from the Open-Meteo Air Quality network, monitoring fine particulate matter (PM2.5) and US EPA Air Quality Index thresholds across global urban centers.

Fine particulate matter (PM2.5) consists of inhalable pollutant droplets and particles 2.5 micrometers and smaller. Elevated readings directly modulate the poster's wave frequency, amplitude, and surface grain.

In 6-Location Global Mode, all six international coordinate streams are batch-queried via Open-Meteo, assigning each city its own typographic terrace step (1–6) and split-strip architectural slice.
