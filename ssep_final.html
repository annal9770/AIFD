Mission Patch Showcase

Baruch SSEP 2027 Mission Patch

The patch represents Baruch's potential and community through the radiating, interlocking stars. The stars represent the individuality of each student and member of Baruch. With each star interlocking, it represents the community aspect of Baruch and how we work together as a collective to achieve beyond what can be done individually. The radiation and repeating lines represent the potential of Baruch as a whole, branching outwards beyond Baruch. The radiating design alludes to how Baruch would also work with other members beyond Baruch. In this case, Baruch would be working with others in Mission 22 of the Student Spaceflight Experiments Program. In addition, there is an exhaust-like design on the bottom left corner. This imitates the rocket exhaust smoke that propels the rocket. This design reflects Baruch's participation in scientific research and discovery in space by visually showing Baruch "cometing" into space, bringing its community along. Pulling together the entire design is its cosmic design of stars to reflect the nature of this mission in space.
AI was used in this project mainly to translate my hand-drawn patch sketch into a clean vector patch. This was later used to generate versions from the sketch. It was also used to brainstorm ideas for how I could create alternate versions and add new elements to the patch.
