Master of Science in Historic Preservation

The master's degree historic preservation program provides specialized training for the individual who wishes to be professionally active in preservation as architects, conservators, historians, and planners.

There is also a four-year joint degree program offering qualified students the opportunity to work simultaneously toward master's degrees in architecture and historic preservation.

As part of the Graduate School of Architecture and Planning, the program draws upon the resources of its master's programs in architecture, real estate development, and urban planning.
